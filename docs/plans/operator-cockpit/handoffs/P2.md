## Interface delivered (P7 to P2)

`mountScene(host, {store, actions, motion, signal}) => close`, as frozen in CONTRACTS. `close()` is synchronous, safe twice and also runs on `signal` abort. `host` should have a definite size (the scene fills it). Behavior the contract left open, all additive:

- `actions.select({sessionId, agentId})` is the only call the scene makes. A pointer pick, Enter on the list and a row click all use it. Nothing else is sent.
- Optional `actions.getSelection()` / `subscribeSelection(fn)` supply the selection to outline. Without them the scene keeps its own selection. `close.setSelection(sel)` does the same imperatively.
- Optional `actions.getDiscreet()` / `subscribeDiscreet(fn)`: aliases (`Project n`, `Session n`, `Agent n`), no tools, reasons or provider, no titles, no `title` attributes, no entity IDs in the DOM. `getProject()` / `subscribeProject()` scope the scene to one project; the camera refits.
- `motion.rm` and `motion.on` are read when present (reduced motion makes the camera jump instead of tween).
- `options.loadThree` (default: the vendor import) lets a shell or test inject the loader.
- `close` also carries `reset()`, `retry()`, `refresh()`, `stats()`, `screenOf(key)`, `worldOf(key)`, `keyOf(sessionId, agentId)`, `getMode()` and `root`. They exist for the harness and for P7's smoke test.
- Store events handled: `connection` (dims the scene, no frame) and `snapshot`. `feed` is ignored. The real store's event types were read from `src/web/data/store.mjs` usage in `network.mjs`; the real store was not run against the scene (the fixture store has the same interface).
- Module graph for the allowlist (static imports of `scene.mjs`): `cockpit/layout.mjs`, `cockpit/projection.mjs`, `components/dom.mjs`, `components/format.mjs`, `components/status.mjs`, and through `projection.mjs`: `components/sessions.mjs`, `data/selectors.mjs`. Plus `cockpit/scene.css`, and the two vendor modules on demand. MIME `text/javascript` for modules, `text/css` for the stylesheet. `scene.css` needs the foundation tokens already loaded by `hud.css`.
- **Exclude `src/web/cockpit/harness/` from the allowlist and the package.** `tests/fixtures/` stays out too.
- Escape is not handled by the scene (P7/P3 own surface closing and focus return). The scene returns focus to nothing by itself.

## What was built (against the P2 scope)

1. **Pure keyed layout.** A project owns a cluster slot and a session owns a member slot inside it; both positions are fixed functions of the slot number. Newcomers are stable-sorted, then take the lowest free slot. Expired keys free their slot and enter a bounded history (256 entities, 64 groups) so a key that returns gets its old slot if it is still free. Children sit at a fixed offset from their parent, in the direction that stays clear of the neighbouring slots (a function of the parent's slot). Orphans, self-parents, cross-group parents and cycles become roots and are reported as `issues`; chains deeper than 6 are clamped.
2. **Projection separate from drawing.** `projectSnapshot` is DOM-free. Labels are HTML text nodes. Delegation links are solid violet lines, shell linkage (`startedBy`) dashed. Idle sessions of an unexpanded project fold into a `+n` satellite when there are two or more (as in the design study); finished subagents beyond the newest three fold into a satellite on their parent (every one above 40 nodes). Enter or a click on a satellite expands it. The accessible list invokes the same selection callback.
3. **Interaction.** Pointer pick is nearest projected node within a 44 px circle (or its drawn radius), so two close circles are cut at their midpoint. A drag past 6 px (10 px for touch) orbits and never selects; wheel and two-pointer pinch zoom; tilt 20 to 80 degrees, azimuth plus or minus 80, zoom 0.55 to 3.2; Reset view. The list is one tab stop with `aria-activedescendant`; arrows, Home, End, Enter, `+`, `-`, `0`. DPR cap 1.5, `ResizeObserver`, at most one pending animation frame, nothing requested while hidden. The graph diff ignores `snapshot.now`.
4. **Disposal and fallback.** Every callback and GPU resource goes on a disposal stack (`createDisposer`, LIFO, safe twice). A rejected import, a renderer that throws (including after it obtained a context) and context loss all land in the same fallback: the HTML list becomes visible, a banner explains, selection is kept, and an explicit Retry creates one canvas. The mount checks a generation, the outer signal and `host.isConnected` before creating a renderer.
5. **Exit checks** below.

## Failing cases found and fixed during the run

- Label plates wrapping ("NEEDS YOU" on two lines) and the project label truncating: width estimates raised and `white-space: nowrap` on the status row.
- A waiting label with no free candidate in the dense layout (the check the design study asked for): extended candidates for waiting nodes at 60 and 120 px, in all eight directions. Ordinary labels keep the 12 candidates of the study.
- `forceContextLoss()` on an already lost context logged a Three.js warning: now skipped when the context is lost.
- A rotation of the member ring that put two slots on the same spot (Van der Corput rounding): bit-reversal order, so every slot is distinct. A child could land on a neighbouring slot in the all-expanded dense case: children now take the direction with the most room.
- Project clusters crowding at the first rings after the 0.62 flattening of the cluster ellipse: ring step 19 and a platform-overlap assertion over the first forty slots.

## Remaining limitations and unverified

- **Not verified:** a headed (visible) browser window; any browser other than Edge 158; DPR above 1 (the runs used DPR 1; the cap is in the code and asserted as at most 1.5); touch and pinch (code only); bfcache `pageshow`; screen readers (ARIA structure only: listbox, options, `aria-selected`, `aria-activedescendant`); the OS reduced-motion media query (the harness drives `motion.rm`); a real background tab (simulated); the real browser store and the real dashboard (only the fixture store ran).
- The narrow-screen composition of the design study (collapsed project hexagons, sheet below a 112 px peek) is **not** implemented in 3D. At 390 px wide the map is small (nodes about 20 px) and the list is the usable path, but the list is only visible in fallback. P7 should decide whether narrow widths mount the list or the scene; no option exists yet for starting in list-only mode.
- Camera anchoring is within about 1.2 percent of the rectangle width, not exact, because of perspective. The camera refits when new content falls outside the view and the operator has not moved it, so on-screen positions can shift (world positions never do). After the operator orbits or zooms, nothing refits except Reset, a project change, or a width change that only refits the distance.
- Projects get cluster slots in alphabetical order on first render, so Ledger is not always on the left as in the 2D study. This is the stable-sort rule, not a pixel match to the frames.
- Hex marks are flat shapes on the top face (triangle, open ring, check, dash, `+`), so the state reads in grayscale from shape alone, but idle and working are close at small sizes as in the study. Links are 1 px lines.
- Project labels are not buttons; scoping to a project comes from `getProject()`. Escape, focus return and the inspector are P3/P7.
- The frame counters and draw timings count what the scene did; GPU time per frame is only visible through the frame interval.
- Software SwiftShader and native timings are both from headless Edge on one host and say nothing about a lower-power GPU.
- Fonts: the scene uses the foundation font stack; the harness has no web fonts, so label widths on the live HUD may differ slightly (the placer uses width estimates with margin).
