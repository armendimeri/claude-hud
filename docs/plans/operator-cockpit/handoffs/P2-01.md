## Summary

A standalone prototype of the cockpit scene redrawn as light, with the opening sequence. It uses the unchanged
`src/web/cockpit/layout.mjs`, `projection.mjs`, `status.mjs` glyphs and the vendored three 0.180.0 by relative path,
so it shows the redo on top of the plumbing that stays (V4). It reads a synthetic fixture (sparse 3 sessions or
dense 24) or the live `/api/state` of any loopback HUD.

What you see: a hex grid floor that fades into the distance, with range rings and an 8 s radar sweep around an
arc-reactor core at the hub (segmented coil, gimbal rings with tick marks, a wire icosahedron, a light cone and
column). Each project is a thin ring with 120 tick marks, two counter-rotating arc segments and a faint lens fill,
with a light spoke back to the reactor. Each session is an edge-lit crystal floating over a hex pad, on a light
tether, with a light pool under it. Working sessions have an amber spinning arc. A waiting session is red, breathes
on 1.6 s and sends out ping rings. Subagents are smaller violet crystals on curved light links, with flowing dots on
the three newest. A finish sends a green ring and a pulse back along the link to the parent. Drifting dust, a slow
camera drift and mouse parallax keep it moving. A bloom pass, faint scan lines, grain and an edge-only lens fringe
sit on top. All text is HTML: crisp labels with leader lines, the project names, the reactor count, the reticle.

## How to open it

```
node docs/plans/operator-cockpit/handoffs/design/redo/serve.mjs
```

Run it from the repository root. It serves only the redo folder and `src/web/` on 127.0.0.1:7791, and proxies
`/api/state` to a loopback HUD.

- Live (your sessions): http://127.0.0.1:7791/docs/plans/operator-cockpit/handoffs/design/redo/index.html?api=http://127.0.0.1:7790
- Sparse fixture: http://127.0.0.1:7791/docs/plans/operator-cockpit/handoffs/design/redo/index.html?data=sparse
- Dense fixture: http://127.0.0.1:7791/docs/plans/operator-cockpit/handoffs/design/redo/index.html?data=dense
- Other parameters: `select=waiting|first`, `discreet=1`, `effects=0`, `opening=0` (skip) or `opening=1.45`
  (freeze at that second), `sim=0` (no simulated events), `perf=1` (frame readout), `quality=0..3`, `msaa=0|2|4`.
- Keys: drag to orbit, wheel or `+`/`-` to zoom, arrows orbit, `0` resets the view, `R` replays the opening, `D` discreet,
  `Esc` closes the inspector. Click a node to select it; click a `+n` badge to unfold.
- The fixture modes simulate a tool call, a finishing subagent or a new subagent every 2.6 s, so the green pulse
  and flows show up without waiting for real work.

**CORS:** the HUD on 7790 sends no `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` header (checked with
`curl -H "Origin: http://127.0.0.1:7791" http://127.0.0.1:7790/api/state`: 200 with no CORS header), so a page on
another port can't read it. That's why `serve.mjs` proxies it. Only loopback targets are allowed (`?target=`).

## Deviations from the plan

- **DPR cap 1.25 instead of 1.5, and 2× MSAA.** Measured: 4× MSAA at DPR 1.5 put orbit p95 at 33.4–33.7 ms, with
  frames over 34 ms. P2-02 should keep the step-down ladder.
- **The scene renders continuously while visible** (ambient motion), so P2's "draw only when something changed" rule
  can't hold with effects on. With reduced motion or EFFECTS off it could go back to drawing on demand. The study
  doesn't implement that.
- Group labels are placed by the study's own rule. Node labels still use `placeLabels` from `layout.mjs`, with
  widths 16% wider than `estimateLabelWidth`, because real Inter text was being cut.

## Decisions taken (defaults, for you to overrule at O-1)

- Elevation 46° and FOV 30°: lower angles looked more cinematic but squashed the rings and crowded the labels.
- No tool text in node labels. "AGENTS WORKING · n NEEDS YOU" under the reactor.
- The reactor coil turns red while anything waits (DESIGN §8 "the reactor turning red").
- The camera and grid fit the scene rectangle (left of the inspector, between the bars). The canvas fills the window
  behind the glass chrome.

## Known issues

1. **Frame budget has no headroom.** The p95 is 33.3 ms (two vsyncs) at 1920 × 1200 and at 1440 × 900 @ 1.25, with
   a mean of about 18 ms. Runs varied between 16.9 and 33.3 ms with background load. The GPU is the limit (CPU p95
   about 16 ms). Removing MSAA brings p95 to 16.9 ms. Fill (floor shader, light pools, project fills, bloom) is the
   next lever. A profiling switch `?off=floor,pools,fill,dust` exists, but single runs were too noisy to rank them.
2. Measured in headless Edge on D3D11, not in a visible window. A visible window adds desktop composition.
3. In the dense scene, project count lines sometimes cross a neighbouring ring, and one count line touches a node
   label. They stay readable but aren't collision-free.
4. Sparse real data is wide and short because the layout puts the first two projects 40 units apart. The rings fill
   the frame's width and the floor fills the rest. Nodes are small (about 14 px crystals) until you zoom.
5. The inspector is a static frame. History, reply and actions are stubs (P3/P7/P7-01 own them).
6. No keyboard list or screen-reader path in the study. `scene.mjs`'s list stays as it is (V4).
7. Edge processes: an early `capture.mjs` killed only the launcher and left about 90 headless Edge children
   rendering, which skewed the first measurements. Fixed (`Browser.close`, then `taskkill /T`). The leftovers were
   stopped, and every number above comes from after the fix.
